2. Description of Service

Synapse is an autonomous business management platform that connects to Google APIs on behalf of business owners to help them maintain an active, policy-compliant online presence. The platform currently provides:

6. Autonomous Agent Safeguards

Synapse's autonomous features operate under strict built-in policy controls designed to protect your business reputation and comply with Google's content policies:

  • Every AI-generated post and review reply passes through a policy validation layer before publication — blocking medical claims, guarantees, foreign URLs, phone numbers, email addresses, hashtags, emojis, and banned terminology

  • A maximum of one post is published per day to prevent content spam
  • If all generation attempts for a post or reply fail policy validation, nothing is published and the failure is recorded in the audit log
  • All autonomous actions can be disabled immediately via environment variable kill switches, without requiring a code change
