Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 April 2026  ·  Last updated: 19 April 2026

1. Introduction

Synapse ("we", "our", or "us") is an autonomous business profile management platform developed and operated by Trendex Hub. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the Synapse platform to manage your Google Business Profile and related Google services.

By using Synapse, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the platform.

2. Google API Services Disclosure

Synapse's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Synapse accesses the following Google APIs on your behalf:

  • Google Business Profile API — to manage your business listing, respond to reviews, publish posts, update business information, manage media, and retrieve performance insights.
  • Google Ads API (upcoming feature) — to monitor advertising campaign performance, optimise bidding strategies, and manage ad spend for your business.

Data obtained through these Google APIs is used exclusively to provide and improve the Synapse service to you. We do not use it for advertising, sell it to third parties, or use it for any purpose unrelated to the functionality described in this policy.

3. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate the Synapse platform:

  • Google Account credentials — OAuth 2.0 tokens (access token and refresh token) that authorise Synapse to act on your behalf. Tokens are stored securely and never shared.
  • Business Profile data — Your business name, address, phone number, category, hours, services, posts, photos, and reviews retrieved via the GBP API.
  • Review and Q&A data — Customer review text, ratings, and questions retrieved from your GBP listing in order to generate responses.
  • Performance metrics — Impression counts, search query data, and engagement metrics from Google Business Profile Insights.
  • Audit log data — A record of autonomous actions taken by the Synapse agent (posts published, replies sent, actions skipped), stored locally for your review.
  • Google Ads campaign data (upcoming) — Campaign names, budget, bid data, and performance metrics necessary to provide advertising management features.

4. How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used solely to provide the Synapse service:

  • Authenticating your account and maintaining your Google API session
  • Autonomously publishing daily business posts to your Google Business Profile
  • Drafting and submitting replies to 4★ and 5★ customer reviews on your behalf
  • Retrieving and displaying your business performance data in the Synapse dashboard
  • Logging autonomous actions for your review and audit purposes
  • Improving the accuracy and quality of AI-generated content

We do not use your data to train machine learning models, serve advertisements, or share it with any third party other than the Google APIs required to operate the service.

5. Data Storage and Security

Synapse is deployed on Vercel's infrastructure. Your OAuth tokens and API keys are stored as encrypted environment variables. Business data retrieved from Google APIs is processed in-memory during each request and is not persisted to a third-party database.

The autonomous audit log is maintained as a ring buffer (approximately 500 entries) in server memory and is cleared on each deployment. No sensitive personal data of your customers is stored.

We implement industry-standard security practices including API key authentication for all platform endpoints, HTTPS encryption for all communications, and strict access controls.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your API credentials and configuration for as long as your account is active. To request deletion of your data:

  • Revoke access — Remove Synapse from your Google Account's authorised applications at myaccount.google.com/permissions. This immediately invalidates all tokens.
  • Contact us — Email info@trendexhub.com to request removal of any stored configuration data.

Upon receiving a deletion request, we will remove all stored credentials and configuration within 30 days.

7. Third-Party Services

Synapse uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Google LLC — Google Business Profile API, Google Ads API, Google OAuth 2.0. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini API — Used to generate AI-written post content and review reply drafts. Prompts include anonymised business context only; no customer personal data is sent.
  • Vercel Inc. — Hosting and serverless deployment infrastructure.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data Synapse holds about your business
  • Correct any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data (see Section 6)
  • Withdraw consent by revoking Google API access at any time
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format

9. Children's Privacy

Synapse is a business management tool and is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Synapse following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
